About LG Recycling

Where recycling meets rehabilitation.

LG Recycling stands for Langi Ghiran Recycling: a Recycle Group soil-recycling and land-rehabilitation operation near Warrak and Ararat in western Victoria, on a property adjoining the western boundary of Langi Ghiran State Park.

Why we exist

LG Recycling wasn't set up to be just another recycling facility. It was set up because this property has a story, and it deserves a future.

Our story

The property was historically used for sand extraction, as a sand mine and quarry. It was operated by the City of Ararat. Like many former extraction sites, that activity changed the original landform and left areas that need long-term rehabilitation.

Recycle Group's connection with the property began in 1993. Over more than three decades, that connection has grown into a bigger question:

Could recycling provide the resources and the economic foundation to repair the very landscape it happens on?

LG Recycling is our answer. By receiving and processing suitable soil and carrying out progressive rehabilitation works, we connect resource recovery directly to land restoration.

Instead of seeing the property only through its industrial past, we're looking at what it could become over the next 10, 20, 30 and 50 years.

From sand mine to something special

Extraction was one chapter. Rehabilitation is the next.

For decades, resources were removed from this property. Our goal is to progressively reverse that story:

  • Rebuild
  • Revegetate
  • Rehabilitate
  • Create habitat
  • Improve the landscape

Ultimately, we want the property's future to be defined not by its history as a quarry, but by its value as a restored environmental and recreational landscape.

Our next chapter

LG Recycling deliberately looks beyond the working life of the facility. Our long-term ambition is to rehabilitate the former sand quarry and create a landscape capable of supporting a future recreation reserve and bike park, subject to the necessary planning and environmental approvals.

Where the scars of extraction have softened.

Where vegetation has returned and habitat is expanding.

Where people can walk and ride, and families can spend time outdoors.

Where visitors can take in the landscape beside Langi Ghiran.

If future generations find it hard to imagine that parts of the property were once a sand quarry, the rehabilitation will have succeeded.

What success looks like

Success isn't measured only in tonnes of soil recycled. It's also measured in:

  • Land rehabilitated
  • Resources recovered and landfill avoided
  • Landforms restored
  • New vegetation established and habitat created
  • Community spaces created
  • The condition in which we eventually leave the property

Part of Recycle Group

LG Recycling is part of Recycle Group, an Australian resource-recovery group developing practical solutions for materials that might otherwise be wasted. Across the group, the philosophy is the same: find a better use, recover the resource, reduce disposal, and create a measurable environmental outcome.

At LG Recycling, that outcome will ultimately be visible in the landscape itself.

Our home. Our responsibility.

Respect the past. Recycle today. Restore the land.

We're fortunate to operate beside one of western Victoria's most distinctive natural landscapes, and we intend to leave our part of it better than we found it.
