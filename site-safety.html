Site safety & find us

An active heavy-vehicle site. Please read before you arrive.

These rules apply to every driver and visitor, every time. Anyone who doesn't follow them may be asked to leave the site.

Rules below are adapted from Recycle Group's other soil site. Confirm LG-specific rules, UHF channel and check-in point

On arrival

  • All drivers and visitors check in at site office / check-in point TBC before entering operational areas.
  • Every load needs an approved soil declaration.
  • Loads are inspected before tipping.
  • Follow directions from site staff at all times.

Required PPE

  • High-visibility clothing at all times on site
  • Enclosed safety boots
  • Hard hat in active operational areas
  • UHF two-way radio in all vehicles, on channel TBC

Not permitted

  • Children
  • Dogs or other animals
  • Thongs, sandals, open-toed shoes or bare feet
