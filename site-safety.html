An active heavy-vehicle site. Please read before you arrive.

These rules apply to every driver and visitor, every time. Anyone who doesn't follow them may be asked to leave the site.

Hours & location

Site rules

Every visit, every time.

Brief your drivers before they leave the depot. It keeps the day moving and keeps everyone safe.

Check in on arrival

All drivers and visitors check in at check-in point TBC before entering operational areas.

Approved declaration

Every load needs an approved soil declaration before it arrives.

High-vis at all times

High-visibility clothing must be worn everywhere on site.

Enclosed safety boots

Enclosed safety boots must be worn on site.

Hard hat

Required in active operational areas.

UHF radio, channel 17

A UHF two-way radio in every vehicle, tuned to channel 17.

Follow directions

Follow directions from site staff at all times.

Loads inspected

Every load is inspected before tipping.

No children

Children are not permitted on site.

No dogs or animals

Dogs and other animals are not permitted on site.

No open footwear

No thongs, sandals, open-toed shoes or bare feet.

On the day

What happens when you arrive.

UHF17

Radio on channel 17 in every vehicle, for the whole visit.

  1. Arrive during opening hoursMonday to Friday, 7:30am – 4pm, with your declaration approved.
  2. Check inAt check-in point TBC, before entering any operational area.
  3. Follow directionsFollow directions from site staff at all times, with your PPE on.
  4. InspectionEach load is inspected before tipping. Loads that don't match the declaration may be rejected.
