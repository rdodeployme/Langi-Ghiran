About this tour

Real terrain, not a render.

The landscape is built from public elevation data with satellite imagery laid over it, so the mountain, the woodland and the plains are where they really are. Height is slightly exaggerated to make the landform easier to read.

The bike park is an artist’s concept, painted over the real aerial and laid back onto the real slope: three downhill runs graded beginner, intermediate and expert, a kids' jump park by the lift base, a pulley lift that pulls riders back to the top, and 16 glamping sites off to the side. Lengths and drops are measured from the elevation data along each run as painted.
