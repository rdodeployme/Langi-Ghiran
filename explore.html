About this tour

Real terrain, not a render.

The landscape is built from public elevation data with satellite imagery laid over it, so the mountain, the woodland and the plains are where they really are. Height is slightly exaggerated to make the landform easier to read.

The bike park is drawn on the real slope of the former quarry: three downhill runs graded beginner, intermediate and expert, a kids' jump park at the bottom, a pulley lift that pulls riders back to the top, and 16 glamping sites off to the side. Lengths and drops are measured from the elevation data along each indicative line.
